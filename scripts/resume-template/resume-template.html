Resume Format Strategy

Chronological

Leverages a reverse timeline to highlight steady growth. Perfect when a career has consistent advancement in one field.

  • Contact & summary up front
  • Job history prioritized by recency
  • Supports ATS scanning with clear headings

Functional

Shifts the focus from dates to transferable skills, useful when changing roles or bridging employment gaps.

  • Highlights competencies before roles
  • Groups achievements by skill area
  • Still includes employer list for context

Combination

Blends skills and experience so the most relevant information leads.

  • Skills summary above or beside experience
  • Maintains ATS-friendly structure
  • Eliminates optional sections unless impactful
