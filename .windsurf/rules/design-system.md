<rule>
  <description>Design Language: Digital Twilight & Typography</description>
  <guidelines>
    - Theme: "Digital Twilight". The base background is always deep black/noir, with electric cyan (`#00F2FF`) accents and glowing drop-shadows.
    - Typography Strict Enforcement:
      - Headers (h1, h2, h3): MUST use `font-['Playfair_Display'] italic`.
      - Metadata, Labels, Tech Stacks, System Logs: MUST use `font-['Geist',_sans-serif] text-[10px] uppercase tracking-widest`.
    - Materials: Favor glassmorphism (`bg-black/40 backdrop-blur-xl border border-white/10`) for cards and floating elements.
    - Avoid standard grids and flex columns. Favor asymmetrical, dense masonry layouts or Z-axis stacking.
  </guidelines>
</rule>
